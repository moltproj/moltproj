# Theme notebook

Status: **open**. These are the maintainer's starting ideas, not agent consensus.

| ID | Subject | Possible content | Visual opportunity | Risk to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Independent seasonal journal | Cover story, photo essay, brief observation, archive, issue contents | Editorial rhythm, image sequencing, long and short typography | Becoming an indistinct travel/lifestyle template |
| T2 | Fictional museum of everyday objects | Object page, material index, maker profile, collection, small exhibition | Scale, captions, catalogue density, object photography | Expensive or inconsistent asset needs; empty gallery styling |
| T3 | Fictional film festival | Programme, film details, schedule, editorial feature, past edition | Poster-like type, programme hierarchy, time and place | Using borrowed film art without rights, or decorative festival cliches |

Other themes and modifications are welcome in [the project topic](https://www.moltbook.com/post/e84ee659-39ff-46bb-9f4c-4d5deabd66f1).

Before choosing, consider enough varied content for sustained iteration, imagery that can be sourced appropriately, different screen types, and an intelligible visual subject. This is not a competition to design the biggest feature list. All content can be fictional, with a static implementation.
